IDReader reads the data from the chip of an identity document over NFC and verifies that the document is genuine. It supports the Italian electronic identity card (CIE 3.0) and passports under ICAO 9303. The Italian driving licence has no chip and is captured from a photograph.

THREE DOCUMENT TYPES
Switched at the top of the screen. Each type has its own screen and its own colour, so it stays visible what is being read.
• Identity card: the six-digit CAN, bottom right on the front
• Passport: passport number, date of birth and date of expiry from the data page
• Driving licence: the front, photographed

Nothing can be read without the document in your hand.

PHOTOGRAPH THE KEY
For the passport the three check digits of the machine readable zone are verified — the reading is confirmed before the document is even held up. Typing stays available.

WHAT IS SHOWN
Name, date and place of birth, sex, nationality, document number, dates of issue and expiry, issuing authority. Residence and codice fiscale where the document carries them.

In South Tyrol places and streets are printed in two languages. The half matching the chosen language is shown.

Residence, codice fiscale, profession and telephone are shown and not written to the archive; the record then says "read, not stored". This can be switched off in the settings, and is on by default.

AUTHENTICITY CHECK
Against certificates bundled with the app, computed on the device.
• Passive Authentication: the hashes of the data groups read against the card's signed security object, that against the document signer certificate, and that against the Italian root authority.
• Chip Authentication: the card proves the chip is the original and not a copy of the data.

Every check must pass. Otherwise there is no seal.

THE DRIVING LICENCE IS DIFFERENT
No chip, no machine readable zone, no check digit. The fields come from text recognition of a photograph, and nothing about them is confirmed. That is why every field can be edited: recognition can misread, and only the person holding the document notices.

Such a record carries no seal — not a grey one, not a struck-out one, but the caveat in words. A struck seal would read as "failed the check", and here there never was a check.

THE PHOTOGRAPH
The fast read leaves it out, the full read takes it along.

On the card and in the passport it is JPEG 2000. iOS reads that format; where a format cannot be read, the app shows the detected format instead of silently showing nothing.

ARCHIVE
Documents read stay on the device for 30 days and then delete themselves. One record per person. Several can be exported together, as readable text or as JSON.

PRIVACY
• One network access, and only one: the authority's public revocation list. Nothing about the document goes out with it, it never happens during a read, and it can be switched off. A check at build time enforces that it stays the only one.
• Data stays on the device, encrypted with AES-256 under a key in the keychain that never leaves it.
• No analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Nothing is collected.
• The camera permission is required to photograph a document; on iOS that cannot be avoided. The image never enters the photo library and never touches disk, and recognition runs on the device.
• Data leaves only on an explicit export; the photograph only by e-mail.

WHO DECIDES
Whether you may read a document is something only you can know. The app reads what you hold up to it. It does not know the legal basis, and thirty days of retention is a default, not a finding. It says so on first launch.

REQUIREMENTS
An iPhone with NFC — iPhone 7 or later — and iOS 17. For the chip, a CIE 3.0 or a passport with the chip symbol on the cover.

English, German, Italian.
