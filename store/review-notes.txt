WHAT THE APP DOES

IDReader reads the chip of an identity document over NFC and checks whether the
document's signatures hold up. It is a utility: no account, no sign-up, no
server of ours, nothing to buy.

HOW TO EXERCISE IT WITHOUT AN ITALIAN DOCUMENT

The chip path needs a physical document held against the phone, so please note:

1. ANY ePassport works, not only Italian ones. Choose "Passport", enter the
   passport number, date of birth and expiry date exactly as printed in the two
   machine-readable lines at the bottom of the data page, then hold the passport
   flat against the upper third of the back of the phone. Reading takes about
   five to fifteen seconds; the document must not be moved during it.

2. The driving-licence path needs NO chip and NO document of ours. Choose
   "Licence" and photograph any Italian driving licence, or type the fields by
   hand. This exercises the full interface - result screen, archive, sharing -
   without any NFC hardware at all. Every field stays editable, and the record
   is marked "unverified", because a photograph confirms nothing.

3. On the Simulator there is no NFC hardware. The app says so plainly instead of
   failing silently: "No NFC available - this device has no NFC hardware."

WHAT REQUIRES A DEVICE

NFC. An iPhone 7 or newer. The Simulator cannot exercise the chip path.

DATA AND NETWORK

Everything read stays on the device, encrypted, and is deleted after 30 days.
The app makes exactly one network request: it fetches the issuing authority's
public certificate revocation list, at launch and on request. Nothing about the
document goes out with it, and it can be switched off in Settings. There is no
analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server of ours.
